Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 6, 2026
Last updated: August 30, 2026

Who we are

"A Simple Way to Pray" (the "Service") — including asimplewaytopray.com and our mobile apps — is operated by Hallowed Gains LLC, a Pennsylvania (United States) company ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Your use of the Service is also governed by our Terms of Service.

In short: the Service works without an account; an account is free and optional; we collect as little as we can; we do not sell your personal information; and we do not show ads.

Information We Collect

Account Information (optional): If you choose to create an account, we collect what your sign-in method provides:

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address, profile picture, and a Google account identifier.
  • Email & password: your email address, the name you enter, and your password. Passwords are stored only in securely hashed form, or handled by Firebase Authentication (Google) — we never store your password in plain text and cannot read it.

Sign-in is processed through Google OAuth and/or Firebase Authentication (a Google service).

Phone Number (optional): If you choose to add a phone number and enable SMS reminders, we store your number to send the reminders you request. See the SMS section below.

Settings and activity: Your timezone, notification preferences, prayer streaks, favorites, and "Bible in a Year" reading progress, so the Service can work the way you set it up.

Push notification tokens: If you enable push notifications, your browser or device gives us a delivery token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) that we store so we can send you the reminders you asked for. Dead tokens are removed automatically.

User Content:

  • Prayer Requests: Any prayer requests you submit to the "Prayer Wall" are public and can be viewed by anyone on the internet. Please do not include sensitive personal information — yours or anyone else's — in these requests. You can delete your own requests at any time.
  • Personal Prayers: Prayers added to "My Personal Prayers" are private. They are encrypted at rest in our database and are only shown to you when you are logged in.
  • Feedback: If you send us feedback, we receive the message and any contact details you include.

Usage Data: We use Google Analytics to understand how the Service is used in aggregate — pages visited, device type, approximate (city-level) location derived from IP, and similar statistics. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects device identifiers on Google's behalf; we do not use it to identify you personally, and we do not use advertising features. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies.

Log Data: Like nearly all websites, our servers keep standard request logs (IP address, timestamps, user-agent) for security and debugging, retained for short operational windows.

What we do NOT collect: We do not collect precise location; we do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera; we do not use advertising SDKs; and we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, to remember your preferences (such as dark mode), and — through Google Analytics — to measure site usage. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings; the Service still works without analytics cookies, though you will need to sign in again if you clear session cookies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and personalize the Service (devotions, streaks, reading progress, saved prayers).
  • Create and manage your account and preferences.
  • Operate the "Prayer Wall" and "My Personal Prayers" features.
  • Send the reminders you enable — push notifications, email, or SMS.
  • Respond to feedback and support requests.
  • Analyze aggregate usage to improve the Service.
  • Keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

For users in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (providing the Service you request), legitimate interests (security, debugging, aggregate analytics), consent (optional features like reminders, which you can withdraw at any time), and legal obligation.

SMS / Text Message Reminders

SMS reminders are strictly opt-in: we only send texts if you add a phone number and turn SMS on in your settings. Message frequency depends on the reminder schedule you choose. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop SMS at any time by turning SMS off in your settings, removing your phone number, or replying STOP; reply HELP or contact us for help. Messages are delivered by our SMS provider, Twilio. We do not share your phone number or SMS opt-in consent with any third party for their marketing purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that make the Service run, and as required by law:

  • Google / Firebase: authentication (Google OAuth and Firebase Authentication), database (Cloud Firestore), hosting infrastructure, and push notification delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — Firebase Privacy, Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics: aggregate usage analytics, as described above.
  • Twilio: SMS delivery, if you enable SMS reminders — Twilio Privacy Policy.
  • Email delivery provider: delivers the reminder and notification emails you enable.
  • Crossway (ESV API): Scripture text is fetched from Crossway's ESV API by our servers. Your personal information is not sent to Crossway.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.
  • Business transfers: if Hallowed Gains LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction; we will continue to honor the commitments in this policy.

Remember that prayer requests posted to the Prayer Wall are shared with everyone by design — they are public.

Data Retention

  • Account data, settings, and prayers are kept while your account is active, so the Service keeps working for you.
  • Prayer Wall requests remain until you delete them or we remove them.
  • Analytics data is retained on the rolling windows configured in Google Analytics (typically up to 14 months).
  • Server logs are kept for short operational windows (typically 30–90 days).
  • When you delete your account (see below), we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it for legal, security, or backup-rotation reasons.

Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS), encryption at rest for personal prayers, hashed passwords, secrets kept in a managed secret store, and access limited to what the Service needs. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — please don't put anything in a public prayer request that you wouldn't want public.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you, and request a portable copy.
  • Correct inaccurate information (most profile details can be edited directly in Settings).
  • Delete your personal information (see Data Deletion below).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time for consent-based features (for example, by turning reminders off).
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the rights to know, delete, and correct, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@hallowedgains.com or use the Feedback form. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by applicable law.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual prayer requests and personal prayers in the app at any time. To delete your entire account and associated data, email contact@hallowedgains.com from the address associated with your account, or use the Feedback form. We will delete your account record, settings, personal prayers, prayer requests, and notification tokens.

Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us personal information without parental consent, contact us at contact@hallowedgains.com and we will promptly delete it.

International Users

We are based in the United States, and the Service is operated from the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States and in the regions where our service providers operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date, and we will give reasonable notice of material changes. Your continued use of the Service after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
