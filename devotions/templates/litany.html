The Litany

ⓘ Praying alone
{% call macros.card('The Kyrie') %}

O Lord,
have mercy upon {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}.

O Christ,
have mercy upon {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}.

O Lord,
have mercy upon {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}.

O Christ,
hear Thou {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}.

O God the Father in heaven,
have mercy upon {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}.

O God the Son, Redeemer of the world,
have mercy upon {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}.

O God the Holy Spirit,
have mercy upon {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}.

{% endcall %} {% call macros.card('The Supplication') %}

Be gracious to {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}.
Spare {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}, good Lord.

Be gracious to {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}.
Help {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}, good Lord.

From all sin; from all error; from all evil:
Good Lord, deliver {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}.

From the crafts and assaults of the devil; from sudden and evil death;
From pestilence and famine; from war and bloodshed; from sedition and rebellion;
From lightning and tempest; from all calamity by fire and water; and from everlasting death:
Good Lord, deliver {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}.

By the mystery of Your holy Incarnation; by Your holy Nativity;
By Your baptism, fasting, and temptation; by Your agony and bloody sweat;
By Your cross and Passion; by Your precious death and burial;
By Your glorious resurrection and ascension; and by the coming of the Holy Spirit, the Comforter:
Help {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}, good Lord.

In all time of {{ macros.solo('our', 'my') }} tribulation; in all time of {{ macros.solo('our', 'my') }} prosperity;
In the hour of death; and in the day of Judgment:
Help {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}, good Lord.

{% endcall %} {% call macros.card('The Intercession') %}

{{ macros.solo('We poor sinners implore You', 'I, a poor sinner, implore You') }}
To hear {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}, O Lord God.

And to rule and govern Your holy Christian Church; to preserve all pastors and ministers of Your Church in the true knowledge and understanding of Your Word and in holiness of life;
To put an end to all schisms and causes of offense; to bring into the way of truth all such as have erred and are deceived;
To beat down Satan under {{ macros.solo('our', 'my') }} feet; to send faithful laborers into Your harvest; to accompany Your Word with Your Spirit and grace;
To raise up them that fall and to strengthen such as do stand; and to comfort and help the weak-hearted and the distressed:
{{ macros.solo('We', 'I') }} implore You to hear {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}, good Lord.

To give to all nations peace and concord; to preserve our country from discord and contention; to give to our nation perpetual victory over all its enemies;
To direct and defend our President and all in authority; and to bless and keep our magistrates and all our people:
{{ macros.solo('We', 'I') }} implore You to hear {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}, good Lord.

To behold and succor all who are in danger, necessity, and tribulation; to protect all who travel by land or water;
To preserve all women in the perils of childbirth; to strengthen and keep all sick persons and young children;
To set free all who are innocently imprisoned; to defend and provide for all fatherless children and widows;
And to have mercy upon all men:
{{ macros.solo('We', 'I') }} implore You to hear {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}, good Lord.

To forgive {{ macros.solo('our', 'my') }} enemies, persecutors, and slanderers and to turn their hearts; to give and preserve to {{ macros.solo('our', 'my') }} use the kindly fruits of the earth; and graciously to hear {{ macros.solo('our', 'my') }} prayers:
{{ macros.solo('We', 'I') }} implore You to hear {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}, good Lord.

{% endcall %} {% call macros.card('Conclusion') %}

O God, who despises not the sighing of a contrite heart, nor the desire of such as be sorrowful; Mercifully assist {{ macros.solo('our', 'my') }} prayers that {{ macros.solo('we', 'I') }} make before You in all {{ macros.solo('our', 'my') }} troubles and adversities, whensoever they oppress {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}; and graciously hear {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}, that those evils, which the craft and subtlety of the devil or man works against {{ macros.solo('us', 'me') }}, be brought to nothing; and by the providence of Your goodness they may be dispersed; that {{ macros.solo('we Your servants', 'I Your servant') }}, being hurt by no persecutions, may evermore give thanks to You in Your holy Church; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.
