Tabernacle Explorer

When God brought Israel out of Egypt, He commanded Moses: “Let them make me a sanctuary, that I may dwell in their midst” (Exodus 25:8). Every board, basin, and curtain was built “after the pattern” shown on the mountain (Exodus 25:40), and the letter to the Hebrews calls it all “a copy and shadow of the heavenly things” (Hebrews 8:5) — every part preaching Christ. Tap or click any numbered part of the diagram to see what it was for, and how it is fulfilled in Him. The entrance faced east, so a worshiper walked the path from right to left: through the one gate, past the altar and the basin, toward the Most Holy Place.

Tap or click any numbered part of the diagram — or a button beneath it — to read about that part of the tabernacle and how it points to Christ.

From the Gate to the Mercy Seat

The order below follows the worshiper’s path: in through the one gate, to the altar of sacrifice, to the basin, and then — where only the priests could go — into the Holy Place and at last, once a year, behind the veil. It is the same path the Epistle to the Hebrews walks (Hebrews 9:1–12) to show that “Christ has entered, not into holy places made with hands, which are copies of the true things, but into heaven itself, now to appear in the presence of God on our behalf” (Hebrews 9:24).
